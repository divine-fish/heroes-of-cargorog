### Perception

**Roll Perception.** On a 15+, choose two questions from the list below. The GM will answer them truthfully, and you roll with Advantage when acting on the answers. On a 10+, choose one.

- What here is not what it appears to be?
- Who's really in control here?
- What is about to happen?
- How can I avoid trouble or hide?
- Who or what is my biggest threat here?

### Investigation

**Roll Investigation.** On a 15+, choose two questions from the list below. The GM will answer them truthfully, and you roll with Advantage when acting on the answers. On a 10+, choose one.

- What here is not what it appears to be?
- What happened here recently?
- What should I be on the lookout for?
- What here is useful or valuable to me?
- What do I notice despite an effort to conceal it?

### Insight

**Roll Insight.** On a 15+, choose two questions from the list below. The GM will answer them truthfully, and you roll with Advantage when acting on the answers. On a 10+, choose one.

- What here is not what it appears to be?
- Who's really in control here?
- How is \_\_ vulnerable to me?
- What is about to happen?
- What is my best way in / way out / way past?
